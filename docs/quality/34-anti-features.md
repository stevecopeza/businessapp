# Anti-Features

## What We Will NOT Build

These are deliberate decisions to avoid scope creep:

1. **Full Accounting**: We're a quoting tool, not QuickBooks
2. **Project Management**: No Gantt charts, no task dependencies
3. **CRM**: Basic customer management only, not a full CRM
4. **Inventory Management**: No stock tracking
5. **Employee Timesheets**: Not an HR system
6. **Marketing Automation**: No email campaigns
7. **Multi-Currency**: Single currency per business (future: maybe)
8. **Multi-Business**: One install = one business

## Why These Decisions

Scope creep kills products. BusinessApp excels at quoting. Adding tangential features dilutes focus and adds complexity.

**Exception**: If 80%+ of users request a feature, reconsider.
