# BusinessApp — Product Positioning

BusinessApp is:

- A quoting and agreement tool
- Designed for small service businesses
- Focused on clarity and trust

BusinessApp is not:

- Accounting software
- Payroll software
- Inventory management
- A full ERP
- A sales pipeline tool

Positioning Statement:

BusinessApp helps small service businesses turn real-world work discussions into clear, shared agreements — without forcing them to work like large companies.
