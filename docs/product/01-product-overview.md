# BusinessApp — One-Page Product Overview

BusinessApp is a quoting and agreement platform for small, service-oriented businesses.

It is designed for businesses where work is:
- assessed case by case,
- priced based on judgement and context,
- and agreed through conversation rather than fixed products.

BusinessApp helps these businesses turn real-world discussions about work into clear, shared agreements — without adding bureaucracy or complexity.

## What Problem It Solves

Most small service businesses struggle not with doing the work, but with:
- explaining what is included,
- tracking changes,
- and proving what was agreed.

These issues usually start at the quoting stage and ripple outward into disputes, delays, and lost trust.

BusinessApp focuses on fixing that exact point.

## What It Does

BusinessApp allows businesses to:
- create clear, structured quotes,
- include photos, notes, and context,
- discuss quotes directly with customers,
- record explicit acceptance,
- and handle changes transparently.

Quotes become the single reference point for understanding the work.

## Who It Is For

BusinessApp is designed for:
- owner-operators,
- small teams (under 10 people),
- service businesses with one primary location,
- businesses that already have a website and want it to do more.

## What Makes It Different

- It adapts to how each business works.
- It does not force a retail or product model.
- It prioritises clarity over automation.
- It treats quotes as conversations, not documents.

## The Outcome

For businesses:
- faster quoting,
- fewer misunderstandings,
- more professional presentation.

For customers:
- clearer expectations,
- confidence in what they’re agreeing to,
- better communication.

BusinessApp makes work explicit — and that changes everything.
