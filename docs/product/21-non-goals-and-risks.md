# BusinessApp — Non-Goals & Risks

Non-Goals:
- Full ERP
- Complex accounting

Risks:
- Over-engineering
- Losing focus
- Feature creep

Mitigation: return to the quote.


---

## Known Failure Modes

- Customers who go silent after receiving a quote
- Quotes accepted verbally but not confirmed digitally
- Scope creep after acceptance
- Partial completion or abandonment of jobs

BusinessApp acknowledges these realities but does not attempt to fully automate their resolution.
