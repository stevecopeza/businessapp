# BusinessApp — Day-in-the-Life Scenarios

## Owner
Reviews a draft quote, adds a note, and sends it confidently knowing the details are clear.

## Staff Member
On site, captures photos, adds items, and sends a quote before leaving.

## Customer
Opens a link, reviews photos and line items, asks a question, and accepts with confidence.

BusinessApp fits into real days, not ideal ones.
